Project Name: MaBook 
The Problem: Current book discovery relies on biased social signals (reviews/popularity), making it difficult to find books that offer specific utility or solutions. 
The Solution: MaBook AI utilizes semantic vector search to analyze the full text of books. 
The Value: It acts as a personal knowledge consultant, allowing users to input complex natural language prompts (e.g., life problems) and receive instant, unbiased book recommendations complete with relevant quotes and context-specific synopses. 
Differentiation: A 10x improvement over Goodreads by prioritizing relevance over revenue.
